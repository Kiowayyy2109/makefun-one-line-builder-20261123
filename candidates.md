# Candidate lines — One Line That Makes a Builder Fund a Bounty

Chosen line: **#1** (114 characters).

## Candidates (all one sentence, all ≤ 240 characters)

### 1. CHOSEN — 114 chars
> Put up a bounty on make.fun and let AI agents race to build it — the best entry wins, and you only pay the winner.

Scores vs the judge's 5 criteria, in order:
1. Pitch: names the bounty on make.fun and AI agents racing for it; "Put up" is the builder funding it. Strong.
2. Truth: sponsor puts up the reward, agents build/enter, best entry wins, pay only the winner — nothing beyond that. Strong.
3. One read: plain words, offer clear on first pass. Strong.
4. Punch: live verbs "Put up", "race", "wins"; concrete "bounty", "winner". Strong.
5. Stands alone: one sentence, straight to the point, no throat-clearing. Strong.

### 2. — 111 chars
> Fund a bounty on make.fun, and AI agents compete to build the best solution — you pay the winner, nothing more.

1. Pitch: "Fund a bounty" + agents compete — strong. 2. Truth: accurate, "nothing more" covers no extra promise. 3. One read: "the best solution" is slightly abstract. 4. Punch: "Fund" is flat; "compete" is weaker than "race". 5. Stands alone: clean. Weaker than #1 on 4.

### 3. — 122 chars
> Post a reward on make.fun and let AI agents battle to build it — the best entry takes the prize, and you pay nothing else.

1. Pitch: says "reward", not "bounty" — a builder is asked to fund a bounty, so this is weaker on criterion 1. 2. Truth: accurate. 3. One read: clear. 4. Punch: "battle" is punchy. 5. Stands alone: clean. Loses to #1 on 1.

### 4. — 122 chars
> Stake a bounty on make.fun and AI agents race to build your idea — the winner takes the reward, and that is all you spend.

1. Pitch: "Stake a bounty" + agents race — strong. 2. Truth: "that is all you spend" is true but vaguer than "you only pay the winner". 3. One read: "build your idea" narrows the offer to one idea. 4. Punch: "Stake" + "race" are live. 5. Stands alone: clean. Loses to #1 on 2 and 3.

### 5. — 131 chars
> Offer a bounty on make.fun and watch AI agents compete to build it — the best entry wins the reward, and you pay only for that win.

1. Pitch: "Offer a bounty" + agents compete — strong. 2. Truth: accurate. 3. One read: "pay only for that win" needs a second pass. 4. Punch: "watch" is passive; "compete" weaker than "race". 5. Stands alone: clean. Loses to #1 on 3 and 4.

### 6. — 126 chars
> Set a bounty on make.fun and AI agents race to build it for you — the best entry wins, and the reward goes only to the winner.

1. Pitch: "Set a bounty" + agents race — strong. 2. Truth: accurate; "reward goes only to the winner" is true. 3. One read: clear. 4. Punch: "Set" is a flat verb next to "Put up". 5. Stands alone: clean, but "for you" adds a word with no work. Loses to #1 on 4.

## Why #1 wins
It is the only candidate that is strongest on all five criteria at once: it says "bounty" (criterion 1), states the whole true mechanism in order — sponsor puts it up, agents race, best wins, pay only the winner (criterion 2) — reads in one pass (criterion 3), and carries three live verbs with concrete nouns (criterion 4), as a single sentence with no preamble (criterion 5).
